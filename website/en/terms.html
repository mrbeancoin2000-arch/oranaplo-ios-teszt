1. Service

ÓraNapló is an iOS app for organizing your own Apple Health records and exporting authorized data to Google Sheets. A compatible iPhone, Health access and a Google account for export features are required.

2. Free and Pro

Free: available heart-rate samples exported to Sheets. Pro: export of additional supported health data, historical imports and workout details; available fields depend on your Apple Health records.

3. Price, renewal, cancellation

Pro is intended to cost HUF 1,000/year in Hungary, subject to Apple’s final displayed price. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled. Apple handles payments. Cancel in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Cancellation stops future renewals, generally at the end of the current billing period. Apple’s refund policies apply.

4. Availability and accuracy

iOS controls background execution; real-time or hourly exports are not guaranteed. GPS, activity and measurement accuracy depend on source data. Missed exports may be retried while data and permissions remain available, but access to every historical sample cannot be guaranteed.

5. Health limitations

The app is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat conditions, and is not an emergency monitoring service. Apple Watch does not directly measure the blood-pressure readings shown from Apple Health. Seek immediate care directly for urgent symptoms.
