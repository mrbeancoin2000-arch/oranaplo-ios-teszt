How does it work?

The free plan exports heart-rate readings; an annual Pro subscription unlocks more supported health and workout exports into your own Google spreadsheet. Apple Health access requires your permission. iOS does not guarantee background execution at fixed intervals.

ÓraNapló Pro – proposed price

The app is free to download. The target Hungarian price for the automatically renewing Pro annual subscription is HUF 1,000/year. Apple shows the final amount before purchase. Purchases are not live yet.

Observations, not diagnoses

Health observations use previously recorded data. They are not diagnoses, continuous monitoring, or emergency alerts.
