Troubleshooting

1. Review Apple Health permissions. 2. Open ÓraNapló with internet access. 3. Check the Google account and spreadsheet. 4. Use Restore Purchases for your subscription. 5. Remove personal data from diagnostics before sharing.

Removing data and revoking access

Disconnect Google in app Settings. Delete your exported spreadsheet from your Google Drive. Revoke Health reading permissions in iOS settings. Cancel Apple subscriptions separately in your Apple account.

Purchase issues

Apple handles payments and refunds. Manage subscriptions at Apple subscriptions. For refunds, contact Apple Support.

Emergencies

ÓraNapló is not an emergency service. Seek medical help directly for acute symptoms. Contact your local emergency number if needed.
